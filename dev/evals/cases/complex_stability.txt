3.1 Samples of each batch were placed at the three conditions on the day on which the batch was placed on stability (Table 1). Time 0 is that day; the initial results of a batch are those of its time 0, and apply to every condition.
3.2 The samples at the intermediate condition are tested only for a batch that shows a significant change at the accelerated condition, at each of their time points from the time point at which the significant change is found.
3.3 A significant change at the accelerated condition is any of: a change in assay of 5.0 % of the label claim or more from the initial value; a result for impurity D or for total impurities outside the shelf-life specification; a water content outside the shelf-life specification; a dissolution result outside the shelf-life specification.
3.4 Assay is reported in % of the label claim, impurity D, total impurities and water content in % (w/w), and dissolution as the mean of 6 units in % dissolved after 30 minutes. Appearance complied at every time point of every batch and condition, and is not tabulated.

5 RESULTS OUTSIDE THE SHELF-LIFE SPECIFICATION
5.1 Investigation INV-26-031 was opened on 18 August 2026 for the 6-month results of V20-2604 at the accelerated condition, the first results of the study outside the shelf-life specification: impurity D, total impurities and water content.
5.2 The results were confirmed by retesting retained samples. A laboratory error was excluded: the system suitability, the reference standards and the sample preparation met their requirements.
5.3 Root cause: moisture ingress through the PVC film of the blister at 75 % RH. The water content of the tablets rose with the time of storage, and impurity D, a hydrolysis product, rose with it. At its intended storage of 25 °C/60 % RH the same batch stays within the shelf-life specification.
5.4 Corrective action: the PVC/Alu blister is not to be stored above 25 °C, and its storage statement requires protection from moisture. No action is needed for the HDPE bottle with desiccant or for the Alu/Alu blister.
5.5 INV-26-031 was closed on 30 September 2026. No other result of the study was outside the shelf-life specification.
6 EVALUATION AND PROPOSED SHELF LIFE
6.1 Where no significant change occurs at the accelerated condition, the shelf life may be extrapolated to twice the period covered by the long-term data, and not beyond 24 months.
6.2 Where a significant change occurs at the accelerated condition and no significant change occurs at the intermediate condition, the shelf life may be extrapolated to one and a half times the period covered by the long-term data, and not more than 6 months beyond it.
6.3 Where a significant change occurs at the intermediate condition, or where it occurs at the accelerated condition and the intermediate condition has not been tested, the shelf life is not extended beyond the period covered by the long-term data.
6.4 A significant change at the intermediate condition is defined as at the accelerated condition (3.3).
6.5 The long-term data cover 12 months for each batch. The assay of each batch at the long-term condition decreases by no more than 0.15 % of the label claim per month, so that the assay of no batch is expected to fall below the shelf-life specification within 24 months.

7 COMMITMENTS
7.1 The long-term study of the three batches continues to 36 months, and the results at 18, 24 and 36 months will be reported in updates of this report.
7.2 The first three production batches of each container closure system will be placed on stability at the long-term and accelerated conditions after approval.
7.3 The shelf life of each container closure system will be confirmed against the long-term data as they become available, and a result outside the shelf-life specification will be reported to the competent authorities within 30 days.
